# ClarityBank – Modern Digital Banking Experience

## Overview

ClarityBank is a modern digital banking application designed to make everyday money management simple, transparent, and stress-free. Traditional banking applications often overwhelm users with complex terminology, cluttered interfaces, and unclear transaction processes. Our solution focuses on helping users understand their finances, transfer money confidently, split expenses effortlessly, and save toward meaningful goals.

## Problem Statement

Many banking applications create unnecessary friction for users due to:

* Complex and confusing navigation
* Banking jargon that is difficult to understand
* Fear of hidden fees during transactions
* Anxiety about sending money to the wrong recipient
* Lack of clear spending insights
* Difficulty managing shared expenses and savings goals

As a result, managing money can feel stressful instead of empowering.

## Our Solution

ClarityBank simplifies the digital banking experience through four core pillars:

### SEE

Gain a clear understanding of your financial status with an intuitive dashboard that displays balance, spending, and recent activity at a glance.

### SEND

Transfer money confidently with transparent transaction reviews, recipient verification, and clear fee visibility before confirmation.

### SPLIT

Easily split bills and shared expenses with friends and family without relying on third-party applications.

### SAVE

Create and track personal savings goals with simple progress monitoring and contribution management.

## Key Features

* Clean and modern banking dashboard
* Transparent money transfer flow
* Transfer review and confirmation screen
* Spending insights and transaction history
* Bill splitting functionality
* Goal-based savings management
* User-friendly navigation
* Human-centered design language
* Mobile-first responsive experience

## Design Principles

Our design decisions were guided by the following principles:

* **Clarity First** – Important financial information should be instantly understandable.
* **Transparency Always** – Users should never encounter hidden fees or unclear charges.
* **Confidence in Actions** – Every financial action should feel safe and predictable.
* **Simplicity Over Complexity** – Reduce cognitive load by eliminating unnecessary steps.
* **Professional Yet Friendly** – Maintain trust while creating an approachable experience.
